How Corteza works

Your meetings, without taking notes

Corteza reads the transcripts and Gemini notes of your Google Meet meetings and saves what matters:

  • gavelDecisions, with the reason behind them
  • task_altAction items, with owner and due date
  • helpOpen questions and risks

Turn on transcripts or Gemini's "Take notes for me" in your meetings. Outcomes of your meetings are private to you.
